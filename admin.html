How many percentile points to subtract from a college's cut-off for each category. Makes more colleges accessible for reserved categories. Keep General at 0. Enter negative numbers.
Slot Difficulty Offsets
How many points to add to scores from each slot to compensate for difficulty differences. Slot 1 (easiest) = 0. Slot 2 (hardest) = larger number like 4.
💡 Example: Slot 2 offset = 4 means a raw score of 80 in Slot 2 is treated as 84 for percentile calculation.
